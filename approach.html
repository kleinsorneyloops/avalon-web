How we work

Thoughtful advice takes time. We take it.

We don't operate on volume. We work with a focused group of clients and give each one the attention their situation deserves.

The approach

We listen before we recommend.

Every engagement starts with understanding, not a quote. We want to know who depends on you, what you've built, what you're working toward, and what keeps you up at night. That context shapes everything that follows.


From there, we do the work — comparing products across carriers, modeling out scenarios, translating options into plain language. By the time we make a recommendation, you'll understand exactly why.


Clients don't leave our conversations with a policy they don't understand. That's not how this should work, and it's not how we work.

The team

Abigail Klein

Advisor & Co-Founder

"I spent eleven years watching what happens to families when there's no plan. I'm not willing to let that happen to mine — or yours."

Abby came to financial planning from a place most advisors haven't been. As a surgical and traveling registered nurse for over a decade, she sat with families in operating room waiting rooms, in ICUs, in the quiet aftermath of the worst days of their lives. She watched spouses scramble to understand accounts that were never in their name. She watched families lose homes they hadn't thought to protect.


Those experiences didn't make her cynical about insurance. They made her certain that it matters — and certain that most people are one preventable conversation away from being okay.


Her particular focus is on the communities that have been left out of the financial planning conversation: women who've been handed a policy they didn't choose, and healthcare workers who understand mortality better than anyone but have been too busy taking care of others to take care of themselves. She speaks both languages because she's lived them.

KC Jones

Advisor & Co-Founder

"Playing center, your whole job is protecting the person behind you. That hasn't changed."

KC's professional career was built on a single principle: protect what matters. As a center, he stood between his quarterback and everything coming at him. It's a role that requires understanding the full picture — what's at risk, what's coming, and what needs to happen to keep the important thing safe.


That instinct carried directly into financial planning. KC's clients tend to be people with real assets and real exposure: athletes who've earned quickly and need their wealth to last a lifetime, business owners who've built something they haven't yet protected, and high earners whose families depend on an income that isn't guaranteed to continue.


His network across Colorado and his deep connections in the sports world give him access to clients others can't reach — and his background gives him credibility with people who've heard too many pitches to trust easily. He doesn't pitch. He plans.

Michael

Senior Advisor

Clients rarely hear from Michael first. They benefit from his work every time.

Michael has spent thirty years doing the kind of work that isn't visible to clients but is present in everything they receive. He holds the Chartered Financial Consultant (ChFC) and Chartered Life Underwriter (CLU) designations — the two most demanding credentials in the industry — and is licensed in more than a dozen states.


His role at Avalon is to ensure that what's promised is deliverable. When a case is complex — a business with multiple partners, a high-net-worth estate, a client with a health history that makes underwriting difficult, a multi-state situation that requires careful coordination — Michael is the reason it gets resolved correctly.


He's not the face of the practice. He's the foundation of it. The advisors who bring clients in can make commitments confidently because Michael is behind them.

What we cover

Life Insurance

Income Protection
