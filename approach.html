How we work

Thoughtful advice takes time. We take it.

We don't operate on volume. We work with a focused group of clients and give each one the attention their situation deserves.

The process

We listen before we recommend.

Every engagement starts with understanding — not a quote. We want to know who depends on you, what you've built, what you're working toward, and what keeps you up at night. That context shapes everything that follows.


From there, we do the work of comparing products across carriers, modeling out scenarios, and translating the options into plain language. By the time we make a recommendation, you'll understand exactly why.

What we cover

Life Insurance

Term, whole life, and indexed universal life — matched to your timeline, your goals, and your budget. We shop across carriers to find the right fit.

Income Protection

Disability income coverage for individuals, business owners, and independent contractors whose income depends on their ability to work.

Business Coverage

Key person insurance, buy-sell agreements, and business continuation planning for owners who need their company to survive the unexpected.

Who we work with

We work best with people who want to understand, not just be told.

Our clients tend to be individuals and families who've been putting this off because they weren't sure where to start — and business owners who know they need a plan but haven't found the right person to help build one.


We also work with people in transitions: a new home, a growing family, a new business partner, a career change. These are the moments when coverage gaps become expensive, and when the right advice matters most.


We are licensed in Colorado and California and work with clients across both states.
