The Discover Process

Know where you stand.

Before we recommend anything, we want to understand your situation. The Discover process is a short conversation — about ten minutes — that gives us what we need to give you something genuinely useful in return.

What to expect

Questions that matter, not questions that fill a form.

We'll ask about your life — who depends on you, what you've built, what your goals are. Not to gather data, but because coverage that actually fits your situation looks very different from coverage that was built for someone else.


At the end, you'll receive a personal summary: where your exposure is, what the gaps look like, and what the right next step might be. It's yours to keep, share with a partner, or bring to a conversation with us.


No cost. No obligation. No follow-up calls unless you want them.
